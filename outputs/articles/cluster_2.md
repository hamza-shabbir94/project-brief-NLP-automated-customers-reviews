### Buyer's Guide: Cluster 2 E-readers

#### In Short
Cluster 2 includes six different models of e-readers, each offering unique features and pricing points. Among them, the Kindle Oasis stands out due to its premium leather cover and high-resolution display, while the All-New Kindle offers a sleek black design and glare-free touchscreen. The Kindle Lighted Leather Cover, despite its attractive appearance, struggles with charging issues.

#### Top Picks
**Kindle Oasis E-reader with Leather Charging Cover**
- **Rating:** 4.61 stars  
- **Number of Reviews:** 67  
- **Praise:** 
  - "This is my 4th kindle and is easily the best one I've ever had. It is very comfortable to hold and is very compact. I was surprised how small it is but has the same screen size as my other kindles. It may look costly at first but remember you are getting a cover with additional battery."
  - "I purchased this as a gift for my wife and she loves it. She has an older Kindle, and she can't believe how much better the Oasis is."
- **Complaints:** 
  - "Easy to carry in purse or pocket but doesn't do anything better than the cheapest Kindle reader."
  - "The Kindle unit itself is rather nice, the auto rotate and adjust being the best part. I returned this item because the cover is flimsy. and I was afraid I would drop it and break the kindle."

**All-New Kindle E-reader**
- **Rating:** 4.43 stars

  - "Great charger. Somewhat pricey however. If you attach it to the cable that comes with the e-book reader, this allows you to plug it into a wall socket."
- **Complaints:** 
  - "Should have been included with the e-reader."

**Kindle Lighted Leather Cover**
- **Rating:** 3.67 stars  
- **Number of Reviews:** 3

  - "I really like the easy setup and the parental controls... it's easy to download games and my daughter loves it."
  - "Good product with great value. Recommend this to all."
- **Complaints:** 
  - "I got this to replace the iPad for a child. I am so disappointed by the charge. It dies so quickly and the charger has to be wrapped around the tablet in order for it to charge. This was after two weeks of having the tablet. Low price but not worth the hassle and disappointment!"

#### Common Complaints
- **Kindle Lighted Leather Cover:** 
  - "It died so quickly and the charger had to be wrapped around the tablet to charge."
  - "The pop-up ads are annoying."

#### The One to Skip
The Kindle Lighted Leather Cover is the weakest product in the cluster due to its poor performance with charging issues and intrusive advertisements. Despite its attractive appearance, it fails to meet expectations in terms of functionality and user experience.

#### Verdict
Among the options, the Kindle Oasis stands out as the most reliable and enjoyable choice, particularly for those looking for a premium
